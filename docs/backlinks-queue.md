# Backlink sites queue

Free places that link to the site itself (directories, tool lists, launch sites, business and social
profiles). Separate from guest posting (docs/outreach/). The backlink researcher routine adds 10 new
sites a day; Claude submits the `ready` ones in Chrome when the owner says "listings"; sites that need
an account wait until the owner signs up (Claude never creates accounts).

Copy for every form: docs/launch/listing-kit.md. Curated software directories (AlternativeTo and similar) decline 'collections of tools': there, submit ILoveMyTools PDF Editor (/edit-pdf) as the product. Never pay, never buy a "skip the queue" or badge, never
add a fake address, never post the same link twice on one site.

**Note on today's run (2026-10-26, USA day):** found only 6 new qualifying sites, not 10, and none is genuinely USA-specific (0 of 6, short of the 6-of-10 minimum) — all are marked International. The reason is structural, not a shortfall of effort: this site has no US address or regional presence, which rules out every real US directory category that actually targets the USA market — local business directories (Yelp, BBB, Manta, Angi — all require a US street address, explicitly excluded by the rules), US-regional startup/tech news trackers that require presence in their covered cities (American Inno/bizjournals, Technical.ly's RealLIST — not fetchable/applicable), and recruiting-first platforms tied to US hiring (Wellfound/AngelList, Built In — paid or hiring-gated, no plain product listing). Meanwhile the easy, famous "USA/English market" sites (Product Hunt, BetaList, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, Crunchbase, GitHub, LinkedIn, etc.) are already used (see docs/backlinks.md Step 1). What's left of "new" USA-flavored candidates for this round was almost entirely low-quality: paid-only (AllTopStartups' free listings are "currently suspended"; Jasmine Directory charges $59/yr plus a review fee; Clutch's free tier shows no website link at all — fake-free), link-farm "submit to N directories" bundles (ListMySaaS redirects to a Launchpedia $99-$499 bulk-submission service — skipped per the link-farm rule), reciprocal/badge-gated (Startup Fame, Dang.ai, Turbo0, LaunchIgniter — still not logged, none confirmed "very high quality" enough for `skip (reciprocal)`), ambiguous/fake-free (Serchen's free tier explicitly has "no link to the company's website"; SelectHub and TrustRadius gate listing behind leaderboard/analyst verification, not self-serve), or simply broken/unverifiable (Slant.co and GoodFirms 403 to the fetcher; BetaPage.co now 301-redirects to PitchWall, already in this queue; Forg.to and SaaSCity have a submit button but no discoverable form target or pricing; 10015.tools, StartupSavant and launched.lovable.app don't resolve/404). The six logged today passed verification cleanly: two are GitHub "awesome list" PRs (awesome-privacy, awesome-pdf) — a new category for this queue, free, no new signup, dofollow, and directly on-topic (no-upload privacy tools; online PDF tools) — and four are general international tool/software directories (10015.io, Crozdesk, NewTool.site, Gets.tools). Recommend next USA-day run try more "awesome-list" GitHub repos for the other categories (image tools, text/word tools, calculators, QR codes) as the most reliable remaining source of genuine free dofollow links, and try a logged-in browser session for the sites that 403'd the fetcher (Slant.co, GoodFirms, TrustRadius).
