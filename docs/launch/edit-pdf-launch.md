## Product Hunt maker comment

Hi Product Hunt! I'm Alamgir, the maker of ILoveMyTools.

Most free PDF editors either upload your file to their servers or only let you stick new text on top.
Our Edit PDF lets you click any line that's already in the PDF and retype it - it keeps the position,
size and colour, reuses the PDF's own font whenever it has the letters, and removes the old wording
from the file rather than hiding it under a white box. Find and replace works across every page.

Everything runs in your browser with pdf.js and pdf-lib, so the file never leaves your device.
No account, no watermark, no daily limit.
