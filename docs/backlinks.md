# Backlinks and guest posting kit

Links from other sites are how Google decides a new site is trustworthy. ilovemytools.store
is new (live since 29 Sep 2026), so it has almost no links yet. This kit has everything ready
to paste; each step needs the owner's own accounts, because listings and emails go out under
your name.

**Rules that keep the site safe (Google spam policies):**
- Never buy links, swap links in bulk, or use link networks or "1000 backlinks" services. Google
  can demote the whole site for that.
- Guest posts must be genuinely useful articles. Link to the site once, naturally (brand name or
  the tool's name), never with stuffed anchors like "best free online pdf merger tool".
- One honest listing per directory. No fake reviews or upvotes.

## Step 2: guest posts (1-2 per month)

**Find blogs that accept guest posts.** Search Google for these and pick blogs that look real
(recent posts, real authors, not stuffed with casino or CBD links):
- `"write for us" productivity tools`
- `"write for us" student tips`
- `"guest post" "small business" tools`
- `"write for us" photography beginners`
- `"contribute" remote work tips`

**Article ideas (each naturally mentions one tool):**
1. *How to send big photos by email without losing quality* (Image Compressor, Image Resizer)
2. *5 private ways to handle sensitive PDFs without uploading them* (Merge PDF, Split PDF)
3. *How many words is a 5-page essay? A student's guide to word counts* (Word Counter)
4. *What time should you go to bed? Using sleep cycles to wake up fresh* (Sleep Calculator)
5. *WebP, PNG or JPG: which image format should you use in 2026?* (image converters)

**Pitch email (send from your own address, one blog at a time, personalised):**

> Subject: Article idea for {Blog name}: {article title}
>
> Hi {Name},
>
> I enjoyed your post "{one of their recent posts}" - especially {one specific point}.
>
> I'd like to write a practical guide for your readers: **{article title}**. It would cover
> {2-3 bullet points of what the reader learns}. About 1,200 words, original and written for
> {Blog name}'s audience, with no affiliate links.
>
> I run ILoveTools (ilovemytools.store), a free set of private, in-browser PDF and image tools,
> so this is something I work with every day.
>
> Would that be a good fit? Happy to adjust the angle.
>
> Thanks, {Your name}

When a blog says yes, ask Claude to write the full article for that blog (send its guidelines).

## Step 3: earn links without asking

- Answer real questions on Reddit and Quora (r/pdf, r/techsupport, r/college) only when a tool
  genuinely solves the question, and say it's your site.
- When the newsletter has subscribers, ask happy users to mention the site.
