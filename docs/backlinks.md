# Backlinks and guest posting kit

Links from other sites are how Google decides a new site is trustworthy. ilovemytools.store
is new (live since 29 Sep 2026), so it has almost no links yet. This kit has everything ready
to paste; each step needs the owner's own accounts, because listings and emails go out under
your name.

**Rules that keep the site safe (Google spam policies):**
- Never buy links, swap links in bulk, or use link networks or "1000 backlinks" services. Google
  can demote the whole site for that.
- Guest posts must be genuinely useful articles. Link to the site once, naturally (brand name or
  the tool's name), never with stuffed anchors like "best free online pdf merger tool".
- One honest listing per directory. No fake reviews or upvotes.

## How to raise DR (Ahrefs Domain Rating)

DR (0-100) measures how many **different websites** link to you and how strong those sites are.
It is logarithmic: 0 -> 10 is easy, 50 -> 60 is hard. What moves it:
1. **Number of unique linking domains.** 20 different sites beat 200 links from one site.
2. **Their strength.** One link from a DR 80+ site (Product Hunt, GitHub, Medium, Reddit) is worth many weak ones.
3. **Followed links.** "nofollow" links (most directories, Reddit, Medium) build trust and traffic but count less for DR. Guest posts and list articles usually give followed links.

Realistic targets: DR 10-15 within 1-2 months (the listings above + 5-10 guest posts/mentions),
DR 25-35 within 6-12 months with 2-4 quality links a month. Never buy "DR 50 in a week"
packages: they are link farms that Google ignores or punishes.
